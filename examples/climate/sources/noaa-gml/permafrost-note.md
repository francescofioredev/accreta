# Observational note — abrupt thaw and the permafrost feedback

*Paraphrased stand-in for demonstration. See ../README.md.*

Syntheses that include abrupt thaw — thermokarst lakes, sudden ground collapse exposing deep
carbon — report a feedback substantially stronger than assessments restricted to gradual
thaw. Estimates around 0.27 W m-2 per degree appear in this literature.

The difference from gradual-thaw-only assessments is not primarily a measurement disagreement.
The two are modelling different sets of processes over different depths.
