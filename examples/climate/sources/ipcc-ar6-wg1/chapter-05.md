## 5.4 Permafrost carbon feedback

Thawing permafrost releases carbon dioxide and methane from soil organic matter that has been
frozen for millennia. Assessed additional release by 2100 spans a wide range, and the central
estimate corresponds to a feedback of roughly 0.09 W m-2 per degree of warming.

This assessment excludes abrupt thaw processes such as thermokarst formation, which are not
represented in most Earth system models.

## 5.5 Remaining carbon budget

The near-linear relationship between cumulative carbon dioxide emissions and peak global
warming permits a budget framing. Approximately 500 gigatonnes of carbon dioxide remain for a
50 per cent chance of limiting warming to 1.5 degrees, measured from the start of 2020.

Budgets for higher probabilities are smaller. The estimate carries uncertainty from the
strength of non-CO2 forcing and from the permafrost response.
