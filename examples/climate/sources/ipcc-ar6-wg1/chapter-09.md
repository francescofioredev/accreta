## 9.2 Ocean heat uptake

The ocean has absorbed approximately 91 per cent of the excess energy accumulated in the
climate system since 1971. Heat content in the upper 2000 metres has increased steadily, and
the rate of increase has itself risen over recent decades.

This uptake is what delays surface warming: the atmosphere adjusts within years while the
deep ocean equilibrates over centuries.

## 9.4 Sea ice and albedo

Arctic sea ice extent has declined in every month of the year. Replacing ice with open water
lowers surface albedo from roughly 0.6 to about 0.07, increasing absorbed solar radiation and
amplifying regional warming.
