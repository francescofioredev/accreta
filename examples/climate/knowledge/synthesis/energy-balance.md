# Planetary energy balance

Earth's temperature settles where outgoing radiation balances absorbed sunlight. Three pages
in this knowledge base cover the parts of that statement.

[[../concepts/radiative-forcing]] perturbs the balance — a positive forcing means energy
accumulating.[^forcing] [[concepts/albedo]] governs how much sunlight is absorbed rather than
reflected, and changes as ice is lost.[^albedo] [[concepts/ocean-heat-uptake]] governs how
fast the surface responds, which is why warming lags the forcing that causes it.[^ocean]

The eventual magnitude is [[concepts/climate-sensitivity]].
