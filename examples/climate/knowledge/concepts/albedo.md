# Albedo

The fraction of incident solar radiation a surface reflects.

Replacing Arctic sea ice with open water lowers surface albedo from roughly 0.6 to about
0.07, which increases absorbed solar radiation and amplifies regional warming.[^ice] This is
a self-reinforcing process: warming removes ice, and removing ice adds warming.
