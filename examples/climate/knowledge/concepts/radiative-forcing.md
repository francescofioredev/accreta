# Radiative forcing

The change in net downward radiative flux at the top of the atmosphere, after rapid
adjustments, measured in watts per square metre.[^def] A positive value means energy is
accumulating in the climate system.

Doubling carbon dioxide from pre-industrial concentrations corresponds to approximately
3.9 W m-2, with an uncertainty of roughly 0.5 W m-2.[^co2]

Forcing is the input to [[concepts/climate-sensitivity]]: how much warming results depends on
the feedbacks that respond to it.
