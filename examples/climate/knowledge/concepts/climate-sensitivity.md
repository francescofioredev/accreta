# Climate sensitivity

The eventual change in global mean surface temperature following a sustained doubling of
carbon dioxide, once the system has adjusted.[^def]

The assessment gives a best estimate of 3 degrees Celsius, a likely range of 2.5 to 4, and a
very likely range of 2 to 5.[^range]

The narrowing relative to earlier assessments comes from combining process understanding, the
instrumental record and paleoclimate evidence, rather than from any single line of
evidence.[^why]
