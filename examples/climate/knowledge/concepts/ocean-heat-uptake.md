# Ocean heat uptake

The ocean has absorbed approximately 91 per cent of the excess energy accumulated in the
climate system since 1971.[^share]

This is why surface warming lags emissions. The atmosphere adjusts within years while the
deep ocean equilibrates over centuries, so the surface temperature at any moment reflects
less than the full response to forcing already applied.[^lag]
