# Permafrost feedback, including abrupt thaw

Syntheses that include abrupt thaw — thermokarst lakes, sudden ground collapse exposing deep
carbon — report estimates around 0.27 W m-2 per degree.[^value]

The note states the difference from gradual-thaw assessments is not primarily a measurement
disagreement: the two are modelling different sets of processes over different depths.[^why]
