# Remaining carbon budget for 1.5 degrees

The assessment reports approximately 500 gigatonnes of carbon dioxide remaining for a 50 per
cent chance of limiting warming to 1.5 degrees, measured from the start of 2020.[^budget]

The framing rests on the near-linear relationship between cumulative emissions and peak
warming.[^linear] The reported uncertainty includes the strength of non-CO2 forcing and the
permafrost response — and the latter is itself
[[contradictions/permafrost-feedback-strength|disputed]].[^unc]
