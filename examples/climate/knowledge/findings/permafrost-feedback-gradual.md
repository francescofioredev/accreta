# Permafrost feedback, gradual thaw

The assessment reports a central estimate corresponding to roughly 0.09 W m-2 per degree of
warming from permafrost carbon release.[^value]

The assessment states that it excludes abrupt thaw processes such as thermokarst formation,
which are not represented in most Earth system models.[^scope] That exclusion is what the
[[contradictions/permafrost-feedback-strength|disagreement]] is about.
