# Planetary energy balance

Earth's temperature settles where outgoing longwave radiation balances absorbed solar
radiation. [[../concepts/radiative-forcing]] perturbs that balance; [[concepts/albedo]]
governs how much sunlight is absorbed in the first place, and
[[concepts/ocean-heat-uptake]] governs how quickly the surface responds.[^src]
