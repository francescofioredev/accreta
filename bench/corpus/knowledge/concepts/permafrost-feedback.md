# Permafrost feedback

Thawing of perennially frozen ground releases carbon dioxide and methane previously locked in
soil organic matter, amplifying the warming that caused the thaw.[^src]

The magnitude is poorly constrained; estimates span a wide range and the process is not fully
represented in most Earth system models.
