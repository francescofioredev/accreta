# Radiative forcing

The change in net downward radiative flux at the tropopause after stratospheric temperatures
have readjusted to equilibrium, measured in watts per square metre.[^src]

A positive value indicates energy accumulating in the system. Doubling carbon dioxide from
pre-industrial concentrations produces approximately 3.7 W m-2.
