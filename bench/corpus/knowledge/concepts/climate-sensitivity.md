# Climate sensitivity

The equilibrium change in global mean surface temperature following a doubling of atmospheric
carbon dioxide, once the system has fully adjusted.[^src]

The assessed likely range is 2.5 to 4.0 degrees Celsius, with a best estimate of 3.
