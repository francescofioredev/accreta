# Albedo

The fraction of incident solar radiation reflected by a surface, expressed from zero to
one.[^src]

Fresh snow reflects roughly 0.8 of incoming shortwave radiation; open ocean reflects about
0.06. Loss of sea ice therefore increases absorbed energy.
