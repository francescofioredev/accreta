# Ocean heat uptake

The transfer of excess energy from the atmosphere into the ocean, which stores over ninety
per cent of the accumulated heat in the climate system.[^src]

This uptake delays surface warming: the atmosphere responds quickly while the deep ocean
equilibrates over centuries.
